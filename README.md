# Ghifty's Bowl — Fruit Salad Sales Analysis

Sales analysis for **Ghifty's Bowl**, my fruit-salad business in Ibadan. I logged every sale in April 2026, then built a Power BI dashboard to see what was actually selling and who was buying.

**Tools:** Excel (data entry, cleaning) · Power BI (dashboard)  
**Data:** 50 orders · 26 customers · 11 selling days (7–30 April 2026)

## Key numbers
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total revenue | ₦119,000 |
| Orders | 50 |
| Unique customers | 26 |
| Repeat customers | 20 of 26 (77%) |
| Average order value | ₦2,380 |

## What the data showed
- **Creamy Fruit Salad drives the business** — 37 of 50 orders and ₦82,000 (69%) of revenue. Whipped Creamy did ₦37,000 from 13 orders.
- **Cups are the winning size** — 30 orders and ₦73,000 (61%). Big Bowls sell least often (8) but at the highest price (₦3,000).
- **Customers come back** — 20 of 26 bought more than once; the top customer ordered 4 times in the month.
- **Weekly revenue fell through the month** — ₦34,000 → ₦33,000 → ₦28,500 → ₦23,500. Best single day was 30 April (₦14,000).

## What I did with it
- Kept Creamy Fruit Salad in Cups as the lead product in promotions.
- Started tracking repeat customers, since most revenue comes from them.
- Next step: add cost per bowl so the dashboard shows profit, not just revenue.
